# RIFT v0.3 — Comprehensive QA Protocol & Verification Suite

This protocol covers end-to-end verification of all tools, rendering pipelines, parameters, controls, overlays, and export routines in RIFT v0.3.

---

## 🏛️ Architecture & Buffer State Reference

RIFT manages four distinct image states:
- **`ST.origImg` (Original Baseline):** Untouched initial file loaded from disk or demo card. Never mutated during normal editing. Target of the red-tinted `⎌ revert`.
- **`ST.img` (Working Base):** The current committed image. Target of transformations, crops, and bakes. Snapshots are pushed to `ST.history` (25 levels).
- **`ST.result` (Live Uncommitted Preview):** Volatile live preview buffer generated by adjusting effect parameters, tone/detail grading, mask blending, or generator live previews.
- **`ST.history` (Undo/Redo Stack):** Navigated via `↶` Undo (<kbd>Ctrl+Z</kbd>) and `↷` Redo (<kbd>Ctrl+Y</kbd>).

## Batch 1: Canvas, Loading, Navigation & Viewport

1. **Clean Initial Startup**:
   - Refresh the page.
   - Verify that **no effect is pre-selected**.
   - Verify that `#pane-params` shows the empty-state box: `⬡ no effect selected`.
   - Verify that the canvas displays the clean drop-hint overlay (`drop image · click to open`).

2. **Image Loading**:
   - Click `✦ sample` in the top bar: verify procedural test card pattern loads immediately.
   - Drop an external image file (PNG, JPG, WebP) onto the canvas: verify image loads crisp and centered.
   - Press <kbd>O</kbd> or click the folder icon: verify standard file picker opens and selected image loads.

3. **Viewport Navigation**:
   - **Wheel Zoom**: Scroll mouse wheel over image to zoom in/out smoothly toward cursor.
   - **Zoom Fit Icon**: Click the fit icon (corners square) or press <kbd>Z</kbd>: image fits neatly within the viewport.
   - **Zoom 100% Icon**: Click the 1:1 icon (circle) or press <kbd>1</kbd>: image snaps to 100% actual pixels.
   - **Zoom Percentage Chip Scrubbing**:
     - Hover over `100%` chip: cursor shows vertical resize (`ns-resize`).
     - Left-click and drag upwards: zoom percentage scrubs upward smoothly up to `500%`, centered on viewport.
     - Left-click and drag downwards: zoom percentage scrubs downward smoothly down to `25%`.
     - Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> while scrubbing: fine-grained precision zoom adjustments.
     - Middle-click or double-click the percentage chip: zoom immediately snaps to `100%` actual pixels.
   - **Pan Navigation**:
     - Hold <kbd>Spacebar</kbd> and drag: cursor becomes grab hand, canvas moves smoothly.
     - Middle-click drag: canvas pans smoothly.
     - Press <kbd>H</kbd> or click `✥` on floating toolbar: pan tool remains active for continuous dragging.

4. **View Comparison Modes**:
   - Apply any effect (e.g. `Scanline Tear` or `Channel Drift`).
   - Click `○ orig`: canvas displays the unedited working image `ST.img`.
   - Click `● result`: canvas displays the processed result `ST.result`.
   - Click `◐ split`: vertical purple split divider appears with `↔` handle.
   - Drag `↔` handle left/right: verify left side displays original and right side displays effect preview in real time without lag.

## Batch 3: Geometry & Crop Operations

1. **Crop Tool**:
   - With crop tool active, click and drag diagonally on canvas.
   - Verify that outer area is shaded and dimensions update live in `#adj-crop-info` and the crop overlay badge.
   - **Aspect Ratio Locking**:
     - Set ratio dropdown to `1:1`. Drag diagonally towards canvas boundary: verify crop box is strictly bounded and never extends past image boundaries.
     - Test `4:3`, `16:9`, `3:2`.
   - **Commit / Cancel**:
     - Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or click in-canvas `✕` button: crop rectangle cancels, image untouched.
     - Drag a new box and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or click in-canvas `✓` button: image crops to region, dimension inputs update, and tool resets to `select`.
     - Press <kbd>Ctrl+Z</kbd>: crop undoes back to full canvas.

2. **Transform (Rotation & Flip)**:
   - Switch to `Adjust > geom`.
   - Click `↺ 90° CCW` and `↻ 90° CW`: canvas rotates 90° and resizes bounding dimensions.
   - Click `↔ flip H` and `↕ flip V`: canvas mirrors horizontally/vertically.
   - **Custom Angle Rotation**:
     - Enter `15` into `[ ∠ 15 ° ]`, select `black fill`, and click `↵` (or press Enter): image rotates with black fill corners.
     - Test `wrap edge` fill mode.

3. **Canvas Resizing**:
   - Locate the inline resize bar: `[ W 800 ] × [ H 600 ] [ ↵ ]`.
   - Verify `[✓] lock ratio` is checked.
   - Type `1200` into `W`: verify `H` automatically updates to `900` in real time.
   - Click `↵`: canvas resamples to 1200×900 and dimension badge updates.

## Batch 4: Effects & Procedural Generators

1. **Effect Selection & Unloading**:
   - Click any effect in the sidebar (e.g. `Scanline Tear`).
   - Effect parameters load into `#pane-params`, row highlights with `.on`, and preview appears on canvas.
   - **Click the same effect row again in the sidebar**:
     - Effect unloads immediately.
     - Row highlight disappears.
     - `#pane-params` returns to the clean empty-state box: `⬡ no effect selected`.
     - Canvas preview snaps back to unedited working image `ST.img`.

2. **Parameter Adjustments**:
   - Click an effect to load it.
   - Drag slider thumb: blue fill bar tracks thumb ball center accurately.
   - Click anywhere along the slider track: thumb jumps directly to position.
   - Click the parameter value number: type an exact decimal value and press Enter.
   - Click `⚄ randomize`: all parameters scramble within valid bounds.
   - Click `↺ reset`: parameters reset to default values.

3. **Interactive XY Mapper**:
   - Click `X` badge next to a parameter (e.g. `intensity`): badge highlights purple (`on-x`).
   - Click `Y` badge next to another parameter (e.g. `frequency`): badge highlights amber (`on-y`).
   - Switch to Select mode (<kbd>V</kbd>) and drag on the canvas:
     - Crosshairs appear with floating value labels (`X→...`, `Y→...`).
     - Sliders update live and canvas reflects the interactive mapping.
   - Release mouse: crosshairs cleanly disappear.

4. **Standalone Procedural Generators**:
   - Refresh page (no image loaded).
   - Click `Perlin Noise` in sidebar: **generator pattern renders immediately on canvas** without needing a pre-loaded image.
   - Tweak `scale`, `octaves`, `palette`: pattern updates in real time.
   - Click `⬡ generate` (or top bar `✓ apply`): pattern bakes into `ST.img`, sets `ST.origImg`, and pushes to history.
   - Click `⎌ revert`: restores original generated state.

## Batch 5: Masking Engine & Range Brackets

1. **Brush Masking**:
   - Open `Mask` tab. Ensure `enable mask blend` is checked.
   - Click `✏` (paint) and draw on `#mask-cvs`: purple mask strokes appear.
   - Click `◻` (erase) and erase part of the mask.
   - Click `◼` (fill all): fills entire mask white.
   - Click `⇄` (invert): inverts black and white pixels.
   - Click `✕` (clear): clears mask to 0.

2. **Light Range (Luma) & Color Range (Hue) Brackets**:
   - Check `light range`:
     - Drag `[` (min) and `]` (max) handles along the black-to-white gradient track.
     - Drag `[` past `]`: brackets flip into inverted exclusion mode (`] min – max [`).
     - Label updates live (e.g. `[ 64 – 192 ]`).
   - Check `color range`:
     - Drag `[` and `]` handles along the rainbow spectrum.
     - Hue range isolates specific colors (e.g. `[ 180° – 240° ]` for cyan/blue).
   - Check both: mask applies strictly to pixels meeting both criteria.
   - Check `edge detection`: turns on Sobel edge mask and disables gradient brackets. Adjust sensitivity slider: blue fill bar tracks thumb accurately.

## Batch 6: Sequence Strip & Preset Management

1. **Sequence Pipeline**:
   - Select an effect and click `+` in bottom sequence bar.
   - Select another effect and click `+`.
   - Click `▶ run`: executes pipeline sequentially and updates canvas preview.
   - Click `★` (save sequence as preset):
     - In-app modal (`#modal-preset-name`) opens asking for preset name with default name pre-filled and highlighted.
     - Press Enter without typing: automatically assigns a descriptive default name (e.g. `Scanline Tear + Dither`).
     - Sidebar switches to `Presets` tab and new preset appears under `SAVED PRESETS`.

2. **Preset Management in Sidebar**:
   - Switch to `Presets` tab.
   - Locate the top creation box: verify `[ preset name... ]` input and `[+ save]` button fit cleanly without clipping.
   - Type a name and click `+ save`: creates preset from current effect.
   - Under `SAVED PRESETS`:
      - Click `✎` (rename) on any preset row: in-app modal (`#modal-preset-name`) opens with current name selected; entering updates preset instantly upon pressing Enter or clicking `rename`.
     - Click `✕` (delete): removes preset from list with toast notification.
     - Click preset name: applies full preset pipeline to canvas.

## Batch 7: History, Revert & Commit Operations

1. **Live Preview Reset (`↺`)**:
   - Apply several slider tweaks on an effect.
   - Click top bar `↺`: uncommitted preview discards, sliders reset to defaults, and canvas returns to `ST.img`.

2. **Red-Tinted Revert to Original (`⎌`)**:
   - Verify the `⎌` button has a distinct red tint border and background.
   - Hover over `⎌`: background transitions to prominent red warning fill.
   - Click `⎌`: completely reverts working canvas back to pristine `ST.origImg`, resets all adjustment sliders, clears masks, and updates canvas dimensions.

3. **Undo (`↶`) & Redo (`↷`)**:
   - Apply an effect via `✓ apply` (<kbd>Ctrl+Enter</kbd>).
   - Rotate image 90°.
   - Press <kbd>Ctrl+Z</kbd> or click `↶`: rotation undoes.
   - Press <kbd>Ctrl+Z</kbd> again: effect undoes.
   - Press <kbd>Ctrl+Y</kbd> or click `↷`: re-applies effect.

## Batch 8: High-Resolution Export & Scripting

1. **Export Modal Dialog (<kbd>Ctrl+S</kbd>)**:
   - Click the export tray icon in the top bar or press <kbd>Ctrl+S</kbd>.
   - Verify modal opens with:
     - Dimension badge: `W × H px`.
     - Resolution Scale chips: `0.5×`, `1×`, `2×`, `4×`.
       - Click `2×`: dimension badge doubles (e.g. `1600 × 1200 px`).
     - Format selector chips: `PNG`, `JPEG`, `WebP`.
       - Click `JPEG` or `WebP`: Compression Quality slider appears (10%–100%).
       - Filename input extension automatically syncs (`.png` → `.jpg` → `.webp`).
     - Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or click `↓ download`: image downloads with selected format, scale, and quality.
     - Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or click `cancel`: modal closes cleanly.

2. **Script Editor (`</>`)**:
   - Click `< / >` on any effect row: script editor modal opens.
   - Click `unlock script editor` and confirm warning: code editor unlocks.
   - Tweak arithmetic in shader function and click `▶ run & save to library`: custom effect executes live and registers in user library.
   - Test `↓ save .rift` and `↑ load .rift` file import/export.
